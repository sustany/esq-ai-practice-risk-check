# Privacy notice: EsqHarness for Claude

Effective November 3, 2026

This notice covers the EsqHarness plugin for Claude and its two servers: EsqHarness (https://mcp.esqcopilot.com/harness) and the ESQ AI Practice Risk Check (https://mcp.esqcopilot.com/mcp). Both are operated by Decentralized Publishing LLC. The ESQ CoPilot privacy policy (https://esqcopilot.com/privacy.html) also applies.

## EsqHarness: what it receives

- The draft text you ask Claude to check for citations and quotations.
- A court name and judge name, and for a disclosure draft the details you give (attorney name, document title, AI tool used, how it was used).
- Your firm code, if you entered one in the plugin settings.

## EsqHarness: what happens to it

- Citations and short phrases from quotations are sent to CourtListener (https://www.courtlistener.com, run by Free Law Project) to look them up.
- Without a firm code, nothing is saved: the result goes back to Claude and the text is discarded.
- With a firm code, each check result (the citations and quotations checked and their statuses, plus the label you gave) is saved to that firm's EsqHarness record, so the firm can show its monthly record. The full draft text is not saved. To ask about or delete a firm's records, email notices@esqcopilot.com.
- Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Remove client names and other identifying details the task does not need before asking for a check.

# ESQ AI Practice Risk Check

## What the server receives

- Your 12 answers to the risk check, each sent only as the letter a, b or c.
- Or the name of the ethics topic you ask about, such as "confidentiality".

The server does not ask for or receive your name, email address, firm name, client names or matter details.

## What happens to it

The server scores the answers, sends the result back to your AI assistant, and then discards them. It does not save answers or results, does not keep a log of requests, does not use cookies, and does not need an account or sign-in. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is kept.

The server runs on a virtual private server hosted by Hostinger. Like any internet service, the hosting network may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver traffic and protect against attacks.

## Your AI assistant

Your conversation with Claude, ChatGPT or another assistant is handled by that company under its own privacy policy. ESQ CoPilot does not receive your conversation.

## The full report link

The result includes a link to https://esqcopilot.com/ai-practice-risk-score.html. If you open it, the ESQ CoPilot website privacy policy (https://esqcopilot.com/privacy.html) applies.
